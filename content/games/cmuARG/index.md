## Background
&emsp;
This project is an Alternate Reality Game (ARG) that was meant to show high school students the different creative technology resources around Pittsburgh. This was also part of a grant meant to test whether or not an AI can facilitate managing an ARG. During the semester, two iterations of the ARG was able to run. The first was created by another group and given to our team of four to run and test. The second was created by our team using the feedback from the first run.&emsp;

This project was part of an internship with Carnegie Mellon University's Entertainment Technology Center.&emsp;


## My Role
My role in this project was the Narrative Designer.
&emsp;

As the Narrative Designer, I was in charge of the lore and other writing in the ARG. I created a storyline and beats that the participants will have to figure out during the run. I also wrote additonal materials such as scripts for certain puzzles. Before each iteration ran, I wrote and sent out preliminary materials to the outside partners we collaborated with. This ensured everyone was on the same page about what would happen during the ARG's run.&emsp;

I also took on responsibilities outside of my main role. I also created assets and gathered physical materials needed for in person puzzles.
