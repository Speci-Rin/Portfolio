## Background
This was a submission for the University of Pittsburgh's Games for Social Impact 2026.v The game jam lasts over the course of about 36 hours. Jammers must conceptualize and build games based on the theme. This year, the theme was "Dolly."

## The Game
My Dolly is a two player couch co-op game for PC where a doll factory has recently entered new management, much to the dismay of the workers. The players must work together to create new dolls, deciding between the company’s requirements and the children' s requests so that they feel conflicted over the role of gender imposed by large corporations.&emsp;

We finished the game jam with a tech demo of our idea that showed off the basic idea of gameplay. Our original intent was to have a "Papers Please" inspired game with an additional person. The gender roles that the corporation gives the player along with the orders themselves are meant to be procedurally generated in a final version of the game. Over time, the player will need to remember more and more company rules if they do not follow their job. In doing so, they may disappoint their customers.

## My Role
My role in this project was a 2D artist.
&emsp;

As one of two artists, I was tasked with creating half of the assets in the game. In total, we made about 60 unique assets with three to four color variations for each asset. I created and designed all items. Then, I drew a version meant to be displayed off the doll before passing it to the other artist to draw the same model on the doll. I also drew the thumbs up assets and large paper asset. &emsp;
